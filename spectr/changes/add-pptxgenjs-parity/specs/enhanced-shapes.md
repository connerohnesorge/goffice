# Enhanced Shapes and Shape Support Specification

## ADDED

#### Scenario: Additional shape geometries
- **GIVEN** a need for specialized shapes beyond basic rectangles and circles
- **WHEN** adding shapes to slides
- **THEN** support equation shapes and mathematical symbols
- **AND** add callout shapes with connector points
- **AND** provide banner shapes and ribbons
- **AND** include flowchart specialty shapes
- **AND** support stars, explosions, and accent shapes

#### Scenario: Shape presets and quick styles
- **GIVEN** shapes on slides
- **WHEN** applying formatting
- **THEN** provide preset shape styles (similar to PowerPoint's Quick Styles)
- **AND** bundle fill, line, and effect combinations
- **AND** support style inheritance and theme integration
- **AND** allow custom style definition and reuse

#### Scenario: Advanced shape effects
- **GIVEN** shapes requiring visual enhancements
- **WHEN** applying shape formatting
- **THEN** support glow effects with color and radius
- **AND** provide soft edges with adjustable blur
- **AND** add bevel and 3D rotation effects
- **AND** support reflection with transparency gradients
- **AND** apply shadow presets and custom shadows

#### Scenario: Shape text improvements
- **GIVEN** text within shapes
- **WHEN** formatting text
- **THEN** support text margins and padding
- **AND** handle text wrap within shapes
- **AND** support vertical text alignment (top, middle, bottom)
- **AND** enable text rotation within shapes
- **AND** handle text overflow (wrap, shrink, clip)

#### Scenario: Connector improvements
- **GIVEN** connector shapes between objects
- **WHEN** configuring connections
- **THEN** support elbow connectors with adjustable corners
- **AND** provide curved connectors and Bezier controls
- **AND** manage connection point attachment
- **AND** auto-reroute connectors when shapes move
- **AND** support connector text labels
