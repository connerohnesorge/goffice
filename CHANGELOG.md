## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Initial implementation of `goffice` SDK for Office Open XML Presentations.
- Support for creating, reading, and modifying `.pptx` files.
- Full support for slide management (add, remove, layout, master).
- Support for shapes, text formatting, images, tables, and charts.
- Support for animations and transitions.
- Office 2016+ strict validation.
- Comprehensive example suite in `presentation/example_test.go`.
